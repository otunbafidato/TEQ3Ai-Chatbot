 Brief Explanation of some of the EveryAI Courses
Track: AI Foundations & Awareness 
 
AI Literacy: Understanding and Using AI in Everyday Work & Life 
 
This one-day masterclass makes AI simple and practical for everyone. Learn what AI really is, where it’s used, and how it can help you at work. Try AI tools hands-on, explore real-world use cases, and build confidence to use AI responsibly in your daily life and career. 
 
AI in Your Industry: Practical Applications & Future Trends 
See how AI is reshaping your sector through real-world case studies and hands-on practice. This course explores current industry use cases, adoption challenges, and emerging trends — giving you the skills and insights to apply AI in your workplace. 
 
Work Smarter with Generative AI: Boosting Productivity Every Day 
Unlock the power of AI tools like ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Notion AI to save time and improve your work. This hands-on course shows you how to use AI for emails, reports, presentations, and daily tasks — giving you practical workflows to boost efficiency immediately. 
 
Master Prompt Engineering: Unlock the Full Power of AI Tools 
Learn how to craft effective prompts that deliver accurate, creative, and consistent results from AI tools like ChatGPT and MidJourney. This hands-on course covers prompting techniques, templates, and advanced workflows — equipping you to get the best out of AI in your work. 
 
Responsible AI: Building Strategy, Trust & Ethical Adoption 
AI can transform businesses — but only if used responsibly. This course equips leaders and professionals with the tools to build an AI adoption strategy grounded in ethics, compliance, and trust. Learn governance frameworks, explore case studies, and create a Responsible AI Charter for your organization. 
 
Ethical AI for Leaders: Making Responsible Decisions in the Age of AI 
AI decisions at the top can shape the future of organizations and society. This course equips executives and policymakers with the tools to evaluate AI initiatives, manage risks, and lead responsibly. Explore global regulations, case studies, and governance models to ensure your AI strategy aligns with both innovation and ethics.
